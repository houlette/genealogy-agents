Look-up requests for restricted FamilySearch films and entries that could not be read online, to be used when the owner visits a FamilySearch Center or affiliate library, or asks a volunteer. Agents draft the rows; the owner decides whether and where to send them, and records in the row that a request was sent, when, and to whom. Check this file before drafting a new request, so the same person is not asked twice.

Add a row whenever an agent meets a restricted film or an image it could not read well enough. Give the film and DGS numbers, the access message the viewer showed, the exact entry wanted (date, entry number, names), what to read, and why it matters.
