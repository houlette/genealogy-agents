# Research log

One entry per search session: date, agent, person, where we looked, what we searched for, and what we found or did not find. Negative results go here too, so the same dead end is not searched twice. Begin each entry with the date and your name, for example `- 2026-11-01 (Gemini) Schmidt, Musterdorf baptisms:`, and end it with the handoff: what is finished, what is next, and what remains unverified.

At the start of each month, move the previous month's entries to `research-log-YYYY-MM.md`, and list the archive files here. Search them with `grep -n` before repeating a search.
