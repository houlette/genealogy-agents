An index of the repositories and record sets this project has used or identified, so that agents do not have to find them again. It lists only what has been found: a place or record type missing here may still have surviving records, so search the catalogs as well (FamilySearch catalog, Archion place list, the archive's own inventory) before concluding anything. Viewer tips and film maps are in `tools/browser_bridge/site-notes.md`.

When you open a record set that is not listed, add a row with the identifier, the years you saw, the line it serves, the date and your name. When you learn that a listed source is wrong or has moved, correct the row and say so.
